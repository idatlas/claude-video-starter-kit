To find stock footage for you, Claude needs a Pexels API key. It's free, and it takes about a minute.

Sign in to Pexels, then open the Pexels API page.

Click Your API Key.

Your key is in this box. Click the copy icon next to it, so you get every character.

Now open your .env file. Paste the key right after the equals sign, and save. Never paste a key into a chat.

Then ask Claude to check your keys. When Pexels says OK, you're ready to find footage.
